Welcome to The Sandwich Bar, the place in Amsterdam for a classic sandwich! With us, locals can still come in for a simple cheese sandwich.

Our Menu

The Sandwich Bar is the place in Amsterdam for a fresh, classic sandwich — made with high-quality, locally sourced ingredients. From a simple cheese to our famous spicy chicken, complemented by fresh juices and barista coffee from Bocca Amsterdam.

Come by for a quick lunch or a relaxed moment with friends. In a hurry? Call ahead and we'll have your sandwich ready.

Catering

Looking for the best sandwich catering in Amsterdam?

The Sandwich Bar is here for you! Our dedicated team of professionals is happy to take care of your office meeting, corporate event, birthday, wedding, or any other occasion with delicious, freshly prepared sandwiches. We deliver our lunch catering throughout Amsterdam (and on request in surrounding areas).

At The Sandwich Bar we believe in quality and variety. Our menu offers a wide range of flavours and ingredients, and we are happy to accommodate dietary requirements — including gluten-free, vegetarian, and vegan options.

Frequently Asked

We offer a wide range of freshly made sandwiches, from a classic beef carpaccio baguette to our well-known spicy chicken sandwich. We also have vegetarian and vegan options available. Our bread selection includes white baguettes, brown spelt buns, and gluten-free bread. In addition to our sandwiches, we also offer one salad, one yogurt bowl (also available vegan), cold-pressed juices, croissants, sweet snacks, and soft drinks.
